# Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes a retail sales dataset to uncover trends in daily sales, customer demographics, category performance, and profitability. The analysis was completed as part of the OIBSIP Data Analytics track.

## Objective
- Explore and clean the sales dataset
- Identify sales trends over time
- Understand which customer age groups and product categories drive the most revenue and profit

## Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

## Key Findings
- **Top performing category:** Electronics
- **Highest single purchase:** ₹2000.00
- **Total revenue analyzed:** ₹911,720.00

## Visualizations

### Daily Sales Trend
![Daily Sales Trend](daily_sales_trend.png)

Daily sales fluctuate significantly throughout the year, with several sharp spikes suggesting seasonal promotions or high-demand periods.

### Sales by Age Group
![Sales by Age Group](by_age_group.png)

The 26–35 and 46–55 age groups contribute the highest total sales, while customers under 18 contribute the least.

### Units Sold by Category
![Units Sold by Category](sold_by_category.png)

Clothing leads in units sold, closely followed by Electronics, with Beauty products selling the fewest units.

### Profit by Category
![Profit by Category](profit_by_category.png)

Despite Clothing selling the most units, profit is fairly close across all three categories, with Clothing slightly ahead.

## Conclusion
Electronics stands out as the top revenue-generating category overall, even though Clothing sells more units — indicating a higher price point or margin per Electronics item. Sales trends show clear seasonal spikes worth further investigation for inventory and marketing planning.
